## 2. Decisions and why
- **Enforce at COMMIT, at the authority.** The single-threaded room authority evaluate the guard against the AUTHORITATIVE doc, closing the check-then-act race. Op-grain refuse. **Now PROVEN live** (P6.6 milestone), not just unit-latent.
- **Belt + suspenders.** Agent picks cold (`coldBlocks`, P6.4) = BELT; authority re-checks at commit (`guardedBlocks`, P6.3) = SUSPENDERS, for the race where a reader moved in after the agent read. The milestone tests BOTH separately: belt over PROPOSED targets (a blind belt would propose band blocks), suspenders over the direct write + the `proposeUpdate` race.
- **`tick()` never rejects; the loop can't be wedged.** Single-flight (`state` read/written across the await → overlap would clobber the fold), + fail-closed outer catch (pre-propose section is unguarded, a long loop must not have tick() reject out from under it), + LRU-advance-on-any-attempt (a throwing actor make `peer.propose` throw SYNC → the `finally` still advances → no livelock).
- **Fail closed everywhere.** null awareness / unplaceable camera / awareness outage → guard whole doc; dropped reader keeps band for `graceMs`.
