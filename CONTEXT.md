## 2. Decisions made and why
- **Insert above the rendered window, not at the camera index.** The interviewer inserts above `order0[6]` (window is `[15,26)`), so the inserted blocks are off-screen-above and the window's top block is preserved — this makes the P0-style `order1[window1.start] === order0[window0.start]` window-continuity assertion valid and keeps the `scrollTop1 = scrollTop0 + INSERTED*30` arithmetic clean (adjudicator hand-verified: `order1[23]===order0[15]`, camera `20→28`).
- **One id-keyed HeightModel reused across both layouts.** `measured` covers only the rendered window (camera ± neighbors) and is keyed by blockId, so it *follows* that content across the insert; the interviewer's above-window blocks are never measured and fall to the estimate — exactly the "variable-height content above, estimates only" milestone condition. Both `computeLayout` and `deriveAnchor` share the one `hm`, so the round-trip is a real invariant, not vacuous.
- **DB durability read-back, not just in-memory convergence.** Snapshot/observe convergence can't distinguish "committed + broadcast" from "committed + durable"; `storeText()` (snapshot + update-log replay) closes that, matching the sibling `headlessPeer.test.ts` pattern, and retires the dead module-level `pool` (now `afterAll(pool.end())`).
